On the Beach has arrived

June 26, 2025 · Keep on keeping on

Death Stranding asked players to rebuild America with their feet. Death Stranding 2: On the Beach asks whether those connections were enough — and what happens when the network stretches past the UCA.

This fan site is a map, not a walkthrough. Use it to meet the cast, compare editions, and find answers before you hoist the next container.

The original game made other players feel like ghosts with good intentions. The sequel keeps that social fabric while putting companions on the trail beside Sam. The loneliness is still there. It just has more voices now.
