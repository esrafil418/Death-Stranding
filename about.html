About the strand

This unofficial fan site celebrates Death Stranding 2: On the Beach — a game about connection, isolation, and the work it takes to keep people linked.

Kojima Productions

Founded by Hideo Kojima, the studio builds original worlds that mix cinema, systems, and social play. Death Stranding asked players to become porters. Death Stranding 2 asks whether those connections were worth the cost — and what comes after the United Cities of America.

The sequel expands traversal, combat, and companionship. Sam no longer walks entirely alone. Fragile, Dollman, and new faces travel with him toward Mexico, Australia, and the Beach.
